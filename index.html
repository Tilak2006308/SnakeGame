MISSION DIRECTIVES & PROTOCOLS

🎯 Core Objective

Guide the Neon Viper through the cyber grid, consuming energy cores to expand your length and score. Avoid colliding with your own tail or lethal arena boundaries.

⚡ Combo Multipliers

Collect energy in rapid succession before the combo timer drains to stack multipliers up to x5 Score Multiplier.

🚀 Turbo Mode

Hold Shift (or the Turbo button on mobile) to accelerate movement speed for high-risk, high-reward maneuvering!

🌀 Portal & Hazard Modes

In Portal mode, blue and orange rifts will teleport your viper across the matrix. In Hazard mode, cyber obstacles spawn progressively.
